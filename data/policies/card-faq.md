Wattle Bank (fictional). This FAQ answers common questions about cards, fees and statements. Staff may share it with customers.

Fees. The Everyday account has no monthly account fee. International transactions carry a 3 percent foreign transaction fee. ATM withdrawals at Wattle Bank ATMs are free.

Statements. Customers can download statements for the last seven years from the app under Accounts, then Statements. Paper statements can be ordered for a fee.

Interest. Credit card interest is calculated daily on the unpaid balance and charged monthly. Paying the closing balance in full by the due date avoids purchase interest.

Lost or stolen cards. Customers can lock a card instantly in the app. A locked card can be unlocked; a cancelled card cannot. Staff should direct suspected fraud to the fraud procedure.
