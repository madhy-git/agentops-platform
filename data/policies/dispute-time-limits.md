classification: internal
allowed_roles: [officer, manager]

Wattle Bank (fictional). Card disputes must be lodged within 120 days of the transaction date. Disputes outside the window are not auto-progressed; send them to a disputes officer with a note (rule R3, reason code DISPUTE_OUT_OF_WINDOW).

Duplicate charges, goods not received and incorrect amounts are the most common dispute types. Gather the merchant name, transaction date, amount and what the customer has already tried.

Refunds above 500 dollars require human approval. Provisional credit may be applied while a chargeback is investigated.
