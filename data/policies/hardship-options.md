title: Hardship arrangement options and eligibility
classification: restricted
allowed_roles: [hardship_specialist, manager]
version: "2026.1"
---
Wattle Bank (fictional). Restricted: hardship specialists and managers only. Frontline officers must route hardship cases to the hardship queue and must not discuss specific arrangements.

Option A: repayment pause of up to three months for customers with a documented loss of income. Interest continues to accrue unless waived by a manager.

Option B: reduced repayments for up to six months, set at an amount the customer can afford after an income and expense review.

Option C: interest and fee waiver for customers experiencing severe hardship, such as bereavement, serious illness or family violence. Requires manager approval.

Eligibility. A hardship specialist must complete the affordability assessment before offering any option. Products that add debt, such as limit increases, must not be offered while a hardship flag is active.
