Wattle Bank (fictional). Restricted: managers and compliance only.

Every complaint starts a response clock of 30 days. Escalate to a team leader if no action is recorded by 50 percent of the window (rule R4, reason code COMPLAINT_CLOCK).

Compensation guide. Goodwill payments up to 100 dollars may be approved by a team leader. Payments above 500 dollars require a manager and a compliance review. Never offer compensation in the first response.

If the customer mentions the ombudsman, treat the complaint as high priority and record the mention.
